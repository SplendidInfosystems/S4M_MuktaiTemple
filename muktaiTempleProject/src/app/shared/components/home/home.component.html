How to Reach:

By Road:

The temple is accessible via road from Jalgaon, Bhusawal, and Muktainagar. Regular state transport buses and private taxis operate along these routes.

By Train:

The nearest railway stations are Bhusawal and Jalgaon, both well-connected to major cities across India.

By Air:

The closest airports are Aurangabad and Nagpur. From there, road or rail travel is needed to reach the temple.
